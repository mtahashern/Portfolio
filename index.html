I built WellSpace to fix that: a free, full-stack wellness web app with daily mood tracking, weekly goal planning, sleep logging, province-specific crisis helplines, and a teacher dashboard with real-time student alerts and class management. 02 / 03
Disaster Safe Home
Structural Eng. Research Prototype
See Images →

Conventional homes transfer dangerous seismic vibrations directly to occupants. I researched real structural engineering principles and built a physical prototype applying base isolation to absorb and redirect ground movement before it reaches the structure. Used real material constraints and load-path analysis to create a functional, research-grounded design. Presented at NRSEF 2026 and won the Outstanding Achievement Award from Professional Engineers Ontario.

What I learned

Applied engineering concepts including base isolation, load paths, and material selection to a physical build. Validated through competitive peer review at a regional science and engineering fair.
