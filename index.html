01
Structural engineering

Disaster Safe Home

Base isolationX-bracingFailure analysisPrototyping See images on LinkedIn
Problem
Ordinary homes pass dangerous ground vibration straight to the people inside during an earthquake.
Approach
I researched materials, stability, and real building constraints, then built a prototype combining base isolation and X-bracing to absorb ground movement and strengthen the structure.
Testing
I uploaded a digital version of the prototype to AI and tested it against earthquakes, hurricanes, and other harsh weather to find the point where it would break. Then I checked how often events that large actually happen in Montreal, Quebec, so the design was judged against the risks of a specific region.
Result
Presented at the Niagara Regional Science and Engineering Fair (NRSEF) 2026 and won the Outstanding Achievement Award from Professional Engineers Ontario.

Software and systems

WellSpace

FirebaseFirestore rulesPIPEDAAI-assisted build Open the live app More on LinkedIn
Problem
Students had no simple way to track their wellbeing or signal distress to a teacher, and sharing anything personal has to feel safe.
Approach
I led the product and directed AI-assisted development, then tested, reviewed, and secured it myself. Students get mood and sleep tracking, goals, a planner, a private journal, and province-specific crisis helplines. Teachers get a live class dashboard with support alerts and student approval.
Safety
Journals are owner-only and teachers see only what students choose to share, enforced by Firestore security rules. Includes an account deletion flow and a privacy policy built around PIPEDA.
Result
Live and free for students and teachers. I reached out to Ontario educators and changed the product based on their feedback.
